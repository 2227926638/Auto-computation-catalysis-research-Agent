# Toy Ru-CeO2 N2 Activation Notes

This is a toy source for pipeline validation, not a verified literature record.

Several reports discuss oxygen vacancy and Ce3+ formation near Ru/CeO2 interfaces, suggesting that oxygen vacancy sites may alter N2 activation and adsorption energy trends.

The exact role of metal-support interaction at Ru supported ceria remains unclear because some studies emphasize interfacial electron transfer whereas others focus on Ru particle size and morphology.

DFT and NEB calculations are often needed to separate N2 dissociation barriers from NHx hydrogenation barriers on oxide-supported metal catalysts.

For ammonia synthesis, NH, NH2, and NH3 intermediates can change the apparent rate-determining step, but coverage and microkinetic effects are often not fully resolved.

Bader charge, PDOS, and charge density difference analyses are commonly used to connect Ce3+/Ce4+ redox behavior with nitrogen activation over ceria-supported catalysts.

Existing simple models may miss realistic interfacial sites, oxygen vacancy distributions, and coverage effects, leaving an open question about the minimum DFT evidence set needed for a publishable mechanism reassessment.
